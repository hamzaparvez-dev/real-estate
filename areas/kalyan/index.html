About Kalyan

Buying and renting in Kalyan

Kalyan is one of the busiest rail junctions on the Central line, where routes to Mumbai, Pune and Nashik meet. That connectivity, plus homes that usually cost less than in Thane or Mumbai, is why so many families and first-time buyers look here first.

Our office is in Khadakpada, so site visits are quick. We help you check what a brochure won't show: monsoon waterlogging, society paperwork, approach roads and the builder's RERA record.

Neighbourhoods

Kalyan neighbourhoods

Khadakpada and Godrej Hill

Kalyan West, near the creek and our office. A mix of newer towers and established societies.

Adharwadi and Wayle Nagar

Dense, well-served Kalyan West neighbourhoods close to the station, schools and markets.

Kalyan East

Tisgaon, Katemanivali and nearby areas, with local markets and a steady supply of new projects.

Titwala and Shahad

Stations on either side of Kalyan with lower entry prices and a longer commute.

FAQ

Questions about Kalyan

Which part of Kalyan is best for a first home?
It depends on budget, commute and schools. Khadakpada and Kalyan West suit families who want newer societies. Titwala and Shahad cost less but add commute time. Tell us your budget and we will shortlist three or four options to visit.
Can you check a Kalyan project's RERA registration for me?
Yes. Send us the project name or MahaRERA number and we will check the registration, the promoter and the promised possession date with you before you pay a token amount.
