About Mumbai

Buying and renting in Mumbai

Mumbai is India's most expensive and most liquid property market. Prices differ enormously from one station to the next, and extra costs such as stamp duty, GST on under-construction homes, society charges and parking all change the real budget.

Many of our clients live in Kalyan–Dombivli and buy or rent closer to work in Mumbai. We build the full cost picture before you visit a single flat.

Quick facts

Rail
Central, Western and Harbour suburban lines
Roads

Metro
A growing network, with more lines under construction
Stamp duty
At the time of writing, 6% for men and 5% for women buying residential property in their sole name in Mumbai (including 1% metro cess). Confirm before you buy.
Neighbourhoods

Mumbai neighbourhoods

Central and eastern suburbs

Mulund, Ghatkopar, Kurla and Powai: strong rail links and many redevelopment projects.

Western suburbs

Andheri, Goregaon, Kandivali and Borivali: established, in demand and higher priced.

Island city

Dadar, Parel, Worli and South Mumbai: premium, space-constrained and heavily redevelopment-driven.

Harbour belt

Chembur, Wadala and Sion: central location and good connectivity.

FAQ

Questions about Mumbai

Can you help me buy a flat in Mumbai if I live in Kalyan?
Yes. We handle shortlisting and site visits with you, check the RERA and society documents, and walk through every cost before you pay a token amount.
What extra costs should I expect in Mumbai?
Stamp duty and registration, GST if the home is under construction, society transfer charges for resale, brokerage and parking. We give you a one-page total before you decide.
