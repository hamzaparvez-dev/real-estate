Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information Prestige Enterprises may receive when you browse this website or contact us about a property.

Anonymous website analytics

We use optional, first-party analytics to understand website usage and improve our pages. Analytics is off until you enable it below. When enabled, we record page views, broad engagement events (such as reaching 50% or 75% scroll depth, spending 30 seconds on a page, and clicking a contact button), campaign parameters in the page URL (UTM source, medium, campaign, term and content), and a referring website hostname when available.

Checking your current preference…

You can change this choice here at any time. Turning it off stops future analytics events from this browser; it does not automatically erase records already collected.

What analytics records

  • Page path and visit timestamp.
  • Referring website hostname, when available.
  • General device category (desktop, mobile or tablet) and browser family.
  • Random session and event identifiers used to count sessions and page views.
  • Campaign tags (UTM parameters) when included in the link you used to visit.
  • Limited engagement event types, such as scroll depth, time on page and contact-button clicks. We do not record typed form values, clipboard contents or keystrokes.

Our analytics endpoint does not store raw IP addresses, names, phone numbers or email addresses, and the tracking script does not read form-field values. These events are intended for aggregate website measurement, not to identify an individual. Device and browser categories may still be technical information relating to a browser. Browser privacy settings, content blockers or network restrictions may prevent analytics requests from reaching our server.

Enquiry information

If you submit an enquiry form, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, mobile number, property configuration, enquiry message and preferences. We use these details to respond to your request and manage the enquiry. Marketing communications are based on the consent choice presented in the form.

Storage and service providers

Enquiry records and consent-based anonymous analytics events are stored using our hosted application and database services. Analytics records are capped to the most recent 5,000 events; older analytics events are removed as new events are saved. Website hosting and browser technologies may process limited technical information to deliver and secure the website.

Your choices and requests

You can disable optional analytics using the control above. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted through an enquiry, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.
