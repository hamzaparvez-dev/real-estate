About us

A calm, careful way to buy property

Prestige Enterprises is a Kalyan-based property consultancy. We help homebuyers explore residential and commercial properties for sale within Kalyan, with clear information and practical document checks.

A practical Kalyan buyer framework

Six checks before you book a property

Use this comparison framework during every project visit. Record the documents and written figures instead of relying only on a brochure or verbal assurance.

Property due-diligence checklist for homebuyers
CheckEvidence to requestWhat to compare
Project registrationProject MahaRERA registration number and project pagePromoter name, registration status, declared completion date and uploaded approvals
Carpet areaUnit-specific plan and agreement for saleRERA carpet area, balcony/terrace treatment and any separately charged area
Total acquisition costCurrent written cost sheetAgreement value, taxes, stamp duty, registration, parking, maintenance deposits and other charges
Approvals and titleSanctioned plan, commencement certificate and title/legal due-diligence documentsApproved use, building stage, land title and any disclosed encumbrances
Possession and agreementDraft agreement for sale and project disclosuresPossession date, delay clauses, payment milestones, cancellation terms and defect-liability provisions
Local site conditionsIn-person visit and society/project informationApproach road, drainage, water supply, access, nearby services and monsoon conditions

This is a buyer's review checklist, not a legal opinion or a substitute for independent legal and technical verification.
