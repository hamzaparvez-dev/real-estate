About us

A calm, careful way to buy property

Prestige Enterprises is a Kalyan-based property consultancy. We help homebuyers explore residential and commercial properties for sale within Kalyan, with clear information and practical document checks.

We work from our office at Prestige One, Godrej Hill, Khadakpada. That keeps site visits quick and means we pay attention to the details a brochure leaves out: approach roads, society records, monsoon conditions and the builder's RERA record.

Every deal gets the same treatment: clear numbers, verified documents and honest advice, in the language you are most comfortable with, whether English, Hindi or Marathi.
