Rent or Buy in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region?

By Prestige EnterprisesUpdated October 29, 20265 min read
Quick answer

Buy if you plan to stay at least five to seven years, can manage the EMI comfortably and have the down payment ready. Rent if you are likely to move, or if the rent is much lower than the EMI and you can invest the difference with discipline.

Look at the rental yield

In much of MMR, the annual rent is only a small percentage of the flat's price. For example, a ₹60 lakh flat that rents for ₹15,000 a month earns ₹1.8 lakh a year, which is a gross yield of 3%. That is lower than many loan rates, so owning costs more than renting each month.

Why people still buy

Ownership gives stability, an asset that can appreciate, and protection from rent increases. For many families the emotional value is the deciding factor, and that is fair.

Questions to ask

Be honest with the answers.

  • How long will I stay in this city?
  • Can I pay the EMI if my income drops for six months?
  • Will I really invest the difference if I rent?
  • Is my down payment tied up elsewhere?

Our suggestion

If you are close, rent for a year while you build the down payment and an emergency fund, and use that year to shortlist locations. We help with both rentals and purchases.

Questions people ask

Is renting wasteful?
Not necessarily. Rent buys flexibility. The wasteful option is buying a home you cannot comfortably afford.
What is a Leave and License agreement?
It is the standard rental agreement in Maharashtra, usually for 11 months, and it must be registered.
