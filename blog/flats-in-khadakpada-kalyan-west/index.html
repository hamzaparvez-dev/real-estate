Where Khadakpada is

Khadakpada lies in the north-western part of Kalyan West, away from the crowded lanes around Kalyan station and the old town. Over the years it has grown into one of the city's main residential areas, with gated societies, newer towers, schools, hospitals and supermarkets. Godrej Hill, on the slope above Khadakpada, is a well-known residential pocket with mostly apartment buildings.

People searching for flats in Khadakpada or flats near Godrej Hill are usually families who want a calmer residential setting while staying within Kalyan West, close enough to the station for daily commuting by rickshaw or car.

Who Khadakpada suits best

Khadakpada tends to suit families with school-going children, because so many schools and colleges are within a short drive. It also suits families caring for elderly parents, who value having hospitals close by, and buyers who want a larger home in a residential setting rather than a flat above a busy market street.

It may suit you less if someone in the household walks to Kalyan station every day, or if you want shops and offices on your doorstep. In that case, compare a central address as well before deciding. Many of our clients visit both areas in a single afternoon to feel the difference.

  • Families with school-going children
  • Households caring for elderly parents
  • Buyers who want a larger home in a quiet setting

Godrej Hill as a micro-location

Within Khadakpada, Godrej Hill has its own character. It is a slope lined mainly with residential towers, so it feels set apart from the main roads while still being a short ride from DMart, schools and hospitals. Higher floors on the hill can offer open views, and buildings set back from the road are usually quieter at night.

When you visit, check the approach road, how easily rickshaws are available at different times, and parking for visitors. These small details shape daily life more than the brochure does.

Hospitals and healthcare

Healthcare is another reason families choose this area. The Prestige One page lists Aayush Hospital and Shwaas Multispeciality Hospital at about 1 km and Fortis Hospital Kalyan at about 4 km from Godrej Hill. Smaller clinics, diagnostic centres and pharmacies are spread across Khadakpada.

If anyone in your family needs regular treatment, time the drive to the hospital you would use at different times of day before you buy.

  • Aayush Hospital: about 1 km
  • Shwaas Multispeciality Hospital: about 1 km

Shopping and daily needs

Daily shopping is easy from Godrej Hill. DMart Godrej Hill is listed at about 1 km, and there are local markets, bakeries, restaurants and banks along the main roads of Khadakpada. For a bigger outing, the project page lists Metro Junction Mall at about 6 km, Mukta A2 Cinemas at about 6 km, Xperia Mall at about 14 km and Viviana Mall in Thane at about 22 km.

  • DMart Godrej Hill: about 1 km
  • Metro Junction Mall: about 6 km
  • Mukta A2 Cinemas: about 6 km
  • Xperia Mall: about 14 km; Viviana Mall: about 22 km

Getting around: rail, road and Metro 5A

Today most residents reach Kalyan station by rickshaw or car for the local train to Thane and Mumbai, so the drive time to the station at peak hour is worth checking yourself. Kalyan Junction is also a major stop for long-distance trains.

Metro is the longer-term change. The Metro Line 5A extension approved by the Centre in August 2026 runs about 11.83 km from Durgadi to Ulhasnagar, with stations including Durgadi, Khadakpada, Bhoirwadi and Kalyan, and an interchange with Metro Line 12 at Kalyan. Construction is targeted for completion around 2030–31, so treat it as a future benefit rather than something to count on for your daily commute in the next few years. What kind of homes you will find in Khadakpada

Khadakpada has a mix of older societies, mid-rise buildings and newer high-rise towers. Resale flats in older societies may offer larger carpet areas for the price but need careful checks on the building's condition, the society's records and any redevelopment plans. Newer projects offer lifts, amenities and modern layouts, and under-construction homes should always be checked on MahaRERA.

If you are comparing configurations, read our guides to 3 BHK flats in Kalyan West and 5 BHK flats in Kalyan West, and our resale flat checklist if you are looking at older societies.

Khadakpada or Kala Talao

Within Kalyan West, buyers often compare Khadakpada with the central area around Kala Talao. Khadakpada is quieter and more residential, with schools, hospitals and DMart close by. Kala Talao is closer to Kalyan station and the old town's markets, and is where Prestige Gold is located, offering 1, 2 and 3 BHK homes from ₹55 Lakh.

If the daily train commute is your priority, Kala Talao may save time. If space, quiet and schools matter more, Khadakpada is often the better fit. Our guide to 1 BHK and 2 BHK flats near Kala Talao covers the other side of the comparison.

Checks before buying in Khadakpada

Whichever building you choose, run the same checks. Look up the project on MahaRERA, confirm the RERA carpet area and possession date in the agreement, check the title and approvals, and ask the society about maintenance, water supply and parking. Visit during the monsoon if you can, or ask neighbours how the approach road and basement cope with heavy rain.

Our guides on how to check a MahaRERA registration number and documents to check before buying property take you through each step, and the ready reckoner rate in Kalyan guide explains how stamp duty is linked to the government rate for your area.

  • MahaRERA registration, carpet area and possession date
  • Title, approvals and occupancy certificate for ready homes
  • Maintenance, water supply and parking
  • Approach road and drainage in the monsoon
