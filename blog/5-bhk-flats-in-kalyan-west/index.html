Why 5 BHK flats are hard to find in Kalyan West

Search for 5 BHK flats in Kalyan West and you will notice how few genuine options there are. Most projects in Kalyan are built around 1 BHK and 2 BHK homes, because that is where the volume of demand sits: first-time buyers, young families and investors. 3 BHK homes are common in the newer towers of Khadakpada, and only a handful of premium projects in the area offer 4 BHK or 5 BHK layouts.

That scarcity is exactly why families who need a very large home often end up looking at Jodi homes. Instead of waiting for a rare five-bedroom layout, they buy two adjoining homes on the same floor and live in them as one. In Kalyan West, a 5 BHK is very often a Jodi home, and it is worth understanding what that means before you start comparing prices.

What a Jodi flat is

Jodi simply means pair. A Jodi flat is two adjoining flats on the same floor, usually a 2 BHK and a 3 BHK or two 3 BHKs, combined into a single home with one main entrance. Some developers plan the combination from the start, so the shared wall, the entrance lobby and the internal layout are designed as one large residence. In other cases, buyers purchase two separate flats and combine them later with permission.

A planned Jodi home is generally easier to live in and easier to document, because the layout was designed for it. A Jodi created later can work well too, but it needs more care with approvals, the structural engineer's opinion and the society's consent, and it may stay legally as two units even though you live in it as one.

  • Planned Jodi: layout designed as one large home from the start
  • Later Jodi: two flats bought separately and joined with permission
  • Usually two legal units unless the plan is formally amended
  • One main entrance, with the second door often closed or converted

Who a 5 BHK Jodi home suits

Jodi homes suit families who want everyone under one roof but still want privacy. A joint family with grandparents, parents and grown-up children can give each generation its own bedroom and bathroom. A family that runs a business from home can set aside a study or office. Families who host relatives often, or who need a room for a full-time helper, also find a five-bedroom home far easier day to day.

They also suit buyers who want the space of a bungalow without the upkeep, security concerns and distance from schools that often come with a bungalow. In a building with lifts, CCTV and a rooftop garden, a large flat can be the more practical choice.

Paperwork to check before buying a Jodi home

Because a Jodi home is usually two units, the paperwork needs more attention than a normal flat. Start with the approved building plan and the MahaRERA filing: check whether the combined home appears as one unit or as two, and whether the wall between them is shown as removable. Then read the draft agreement to see whether you are signing one agreement for a combined unit or two agreements for two units.

Stamp duty and registration are charged on the value of what you register, so two units mean two values on record, possibly two registrations, and two sets of society shares and maintenance bills unless the society agrees otherwise. Property tax may also be assessed separately. None of this is a problem if you know about it in advance; it becomes a problem only when buyers discover it after possession.

  • Approved plan: is the Jodi shown as one home or two units?
  • MahaRERA filing: how are the units listed?
  • Agreement: one combined agreement or two separate agreements?
  • Structural opinion: is the shared wall non-structural?
  • Society: one membership or two, and how will maintenance be billed?
  • Home loan: will the lender fund both units in one loan?

Home loans for a 5 BHK Jodi

Most lenders are comfortable funding a Jodi purchase, but they will want to see exactly what is being bought. If the home is two legal units, the bank may sanction one loan secured on both or two linked loans; ask early, because it affects the processing fee, the legal and technical valuation and the disbursement schedule. A larger home also means a larger down payment in absolute terms, usually at least 10% to 25% of the value depending on the loan amount and the lender's rules.

Get the sanction letter before you sign the agreement, and make sure the lender's technical team has seen the plan for the combined home. Our home loan eligibility and EMI guide explains how banks look at income and existing loans.

How to compare 5 BHK homes fairly

Compare RERA carpet area, not built-up or super built-up area. For a Jodi, add the RERA carpet areas of both units and subtract nothing; then ask how much of that area is lost or gained when the shared wall and second entrance are handled. Compare the number of bathrooms and balconies, the floor and view, and the parking allotted, because a five-bedroom home usually needs two parking spaces.

Finally, compare the total cost rather than the base price. Our guide to the total cost of buying a flat in Kalyan West lists every charge to include, and our guide to 3 BHK flats in Kalyan West helps if you are deciding between one large 3 BHK and a 5 BHK Jodi.
