Prestige Flats in Kalyan West: Prestige Gold vs Prestige One

By Prestige Projects · Updated November 26, 2026 · 9 min read

Quick answer

Prestige Projects has two addresses in Kalyan West. Prestige Gold, near Kala Talao and Mahavir Jain School, offers 1, 2 and 3 BHK flats from ₹55 Lakh plus shops and offices, with a sample flat ready. Prestige One, at Godrej Hill in Khadakpada, offers only 3 BHK and 5 BHK (Jodi) homes, with layouts and prices shared on request.

Two Prestige projects, one neighbourhood: Kalyan West

When people search for Prestige flats in Kalyan West, they are usually looking for one of two projects: Prestige Gold or Prestige One. Both are presented by Prestige Projects, the team behind prestigeenterprises.in, which many buyers still search for as Prestige Enterprises. The two projects are only a short drive apart, yet they are built for very different buyers, so it helps to understand each before you book a site visit.

Prestige Gold sits close to Kala Talao, near Mahavir Jain School, in the older and busier core of Kalyan West. It is a mixed-use project: residential 1, 2 and 3 BHK flats sit alongside retail and office space. Prestige One sits on Godrej Hill in Khadakpada, a quieter, greener residential pocket, and is a fully residential address offering only 3 BHK and 5 BHK (Jodi) homes.

This guide puts the two side by side using only what is published on our project pages, so you can decide which one deserves your first visit. For full details, open the Prestige Gold project page and the Prestige One project page.

Prestige Gold at a glance

Prestige Gold is designed for buyers who want a first home or an upgrade in a well-established part of Kalyan West, and for investors who like the idea of residential and commercial space in one address. The project page lists the following starting prices and RERA areas.

Every price shown on the site is an all-in starting figure for the smallest unit in that configuration. Premium and corner units are priced on request, and the final value for any specific flat is confirmed in writing before booking. That written cost sheet is the document to compare, not a brochure headline.

  • 1 BHK: 404 to 519 sq. ft. (RERA area), all-in price starting from ₹55 Lakh
  • 2 BHK: 577 to 721 sq. ft. (RERA area), all-in price starting from ₹75 Lakh
  • 3 BHK: available across the complete site, all-in price starting from ₹1.40 Crore
  • Commercial: retail and office space, sizes and brochure on request
  • Possession listed on the project page as June 2027*
  • Sample flat ready for an on-site walkthrough

What the Prestige Gold flats include

The 1 BHK plans combine a living and dining room, one bedroom with an attached bath, a modular kitchen provision, a balcony with a city view and a utility or dry balcony. The 2 BHK plans add a second bedroom with a common bath, a dry balcony off the kitchen, a main balcony and UPVC sliding windows. The 3 BHK homes offer a grand living and dining space, a master bedroom with a walk-in wardrobe, two more bedrooms, three bathrooms (two attached), two full-size balconies and a dedicated pooja room.

Common to all flats are a decorative entrance gate and designer lobby, branded vitrified flooring, powder-coated aluminium French windows, designer bathrooms with branded fittings and wooden doors. Security includes CCTV across common areas, a secured gated entrance, branded lifts with power backup and well-lit lobbies, corridors and parking. A landscaped rooftop garden with gazebos, a water feature, an open gym and planting beds is reached directly by lift.

Prestige One at a glance

Prestige One is a different proposition. It is a full residential address on Godrej Hill, Khadakpada, and it offers only two configurations: 3 BHK and 5 BHK (Jodi). A Jodi home is formed by combining two adjoining flats into one larger residence, which is why the 5 BHK option is rare in Kalyan West. Our guide on what a Jodi flat means explains the paperwork side of this.

Carpet areas, floor plans and prices for Prestige One are shared privately with serious buyers rather than published online. That is a deliberate choice for a small number of large homes. If you want the numbers, the fastest route is to request them on WhatsApp or call 96895 57788 and ask for a written cost sheet for the exact unit you are considering.

  • Configurations: 3 BHK and 5 BHK (Jodi) only
  • Location: Prestige One, Godrej Hill, Khadakpada, Kalyan 421301

Location: Kala Talao versus Godrej Hill

Prestige Gold puts you close to the older commercial heart of Kalyan West. Kala Talao, the lake and its promenade, is a familiar landmark, and the surrounding streets have schools, clinics, banks and markets within a short walk. Buyers who value being near shops and everyday services, or who want commercial space with steady footfall, tend to prefer this side.

Prestige One is set on Godrej Hill in Khadakpada, a residential part of Kalyan West known for wider roads, newer towers and more greenery. From Prestige One, the project pages list DMart Godrej Hill, Aayush Hospital and Shwaas Multispeciality Hospital at about 1 km, Podar International School and Vibgyor Roots & Rise at about 1 km, Birla College at about 2 km and Fortis Hospital Kalyan at about 4 km. Distances and drive times are approximate and change with traffic.

Both projects are in Kalyan West, so both share the same station, the same municipal corporation (Kalyan Dombivli Municipal Corporation) and the same long-term infrastructure story. Our guide to upcoming infrastructure near Kalyan covers Metro Line 5 and 5A, which plans stations at Khadakpada and Durgadi.

Which Prestige project fits which buyer?

There is no single best choice; it depends on your budget, your family size and how you plan to use the home. The short list below is how we usually explain it to buyers at the first meeting.

  • First-time buyer or young couple with a budget around ₹55 to 80 Lakh: start with a Prestige Gold 1 BHK or 2 BHK
  • Growing family that wants a pooja room and a walk-in wardrobe: compare the Prestige Gold 3 BHK with the Prestige One 3 BHK
  • Large or joint family that wants one big home on one floor: look at the Prestige One 5 BHK (Jodi)
  • Investor who wants rental demand or a shop: look at Prestige Gold flats and commercial space
  • Buyer who wants a quieter residential pocket close to schools on Godrej Hill: Prestige One

How to compare the two properly

Do not compare a published starting price with a price you have not yet seen. Ask for a written cost sheet for each shortlisted unit and compare like for like: RERA carpet area, floor, view, parking, stamp duty, registration, GST if applicable, and society or maintenance deposits. Our guide on the total cost of buying a flat in Kalyan West lists every line you should expect.

Next, check the registration. The site lists MahaRERA registration number P51700052362 in its footer and FAQ; search any registration number on the MahaRERA portal yourself and read the filing before you pay a token. Our step-by-step MahaRERA check shows exactly where to click.

Finally, visit at two different times of day. See Kala Talao and the roads around Prestige Gold in the evening rush, and visit Godrej Hill on a weekday morning to judge the school traffic. A visit to the Prestige Gold sample flat gives you a real sense of the finishes and room sizes before you decide.
