Why so many families search for a 3 BHK in Kalyan West

A 3 BHK is the size many growing families settle on once they outgrow a 2 BHK. It gives parents their own room, a room for children who need space to study, and a third room that can be a guest room, a room for grandparents or a home office. In Kalyan West, where many buyers move from rented flats in Thane, Dombivli or Mumbai suburbs, a 3 BHK is often the home they plan to stay in for the long term.

The search "3 BHK flats in Kalyan West" brings up a wide mix: resale flats in older societies, under-construction towers and a few ready homes. The two Prestige addresses sit in different parts of Kalyan West and suit different buyers, so it helps to look at them side by side.

What to compare in any 3 BHK

Ask for the RERA carpet area in writing and compare that, not the built-up or super built-up area. Note the size of each bedroom, not just the total: a 3 BHK with one very small bedroom may not work for a teenager who needs a desk. Check the number of bathrooms and whether the master bathroom is attached, the balconies, natural light and cross-ventilation, and the floor and view.

Ask how many parking spaces come with the flat and whether they are covered. Check the lift count against the number of flats per floor, the water source and storage, and the backup for lifts and common areas. Finally, confirm the possession date in the agreement matches the MahaRERA timeline.

  • RERA carpet area in writing
  • Bedroom sizes and number of attached bathrooms
  • Light, ventilation, floor and view
  • Parking spaces, lifts and power backup
  • Possession date in the agreement vs MahaRERA

The real cost of a 3 BHK

The Prestige Gold page shows an all-in starting price of ₹1.40 Cr for the 3 BHK, for the smallest unit in that type. "All-in" is a useful headline, but always ask for a written cost sheet that spells out exactly what it covers, such as GST, stamp duty, registration, parking and society charges, and what is extra, such as home loan processing fees, advance maintenance and your own furniture and interiors. When comparing with other projects, check whether their prices are all-in or base prices, or you will not be comparing like with like.

Our guide to the total cost of buying a flat in Kalyan West walks through each line, and our stamp duty and registration charges guide explains how the duty is worked out.

Under construction or ready to move

Prestige Gold is under construction with possession targeted for June 2027, which means you pay in stages linked to construction and pay GST, but you may have more choice of floor and layout. A ready-to-move resale 3 BHK lets you move in immediately and avoids GST, but you take on the age of the building and need to check the society's records and dues carefully.

Neither is right for everyone. Read our comparison of under-construction vs ready-to-move homes before you decide, and always check a project's MahaRERA page using our guide on how to check a MahaRERA registration number.

Home loan for a 3 BHK

Most buyers fund a 3 BHK with a home loan. Lenders typically finance up to 75% to 90% of the property value depending on the loan amount, so plan your own contribution plus the extra costs above. Get a loan sanction before you sign the agreement, and for an under-construction home confirm that the lender has approved the project or will do a technical check on it.

Compare interest rates, processing fees and prepayment terms, not just the EMI. Our home loan eligibility and EMI guide explains how banks calculate how much you can borrow.

Questions to ask on your site visit

A site visit is the best time to clear doubts, so go with a list. Visit once on a weekday evening and once at the weekend if you can, to see traffic, noise and how busy the approach road gets. Look at the sample flat, but also ask to see the actual floor and direction of the unit you are considering, because light and view change floor by floor.

Ask the team to show you the MahaRERA registration on the official portal, the approved plan for your unit and the payment schedule. Take photos of the cost sheet and note the name of the person you spoke to, so that every promise can be confirmed in writing later.

  • Which floor and direction is my unit, and can I see it?
  • What is the RERA carpet area of each room?
  • How many parking spaces are included?
  • What is the payment schedule and the possession date in the agreement?
