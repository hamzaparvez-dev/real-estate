Only two configurations: 3 BHK and 5 BHK (Jodi)

The 3 BHK at Prestige One is a spacious family layout aimed at households that have outgrown a 2 BHK and want each child to have a room, or need a study or guest room. The 5 BHK (Jodi) is formed by combining two adjoining homes into one large residence on the same floor. It is aimed at joint families, families with elderly parents living together, or buyers who simply want a very large home without moving to a bungalow.

Five-bedroom homes are uncommon in Kalyan West, so the Jodi option is one of the main reasons buyers shortlist Prestige One. Before buying any Jodi home, understand how the combined unit is documented. Our guide to 5 BHK flats in Kalyan West explains what a Jodi flat is and which papers to check.

Why prices are shared on request

Prestige One does not publish prices or carpet areas online. With a small number of large homes, each unit differs by floor, view and layout, and a single headline price would mislead more buyers than it would help. Instead, layouts, carpet areas and pricing are shared privately once you tell us which home you are interested in.

The fastest way to get the numbers is to message us on WhatsApp or call 96895 57788 and ask for the brochure, floor plan and a written cost sheet for a specific 3 BHK or 5 BHK (Jodi) unit. Godrej Hill and Khadakpada: the neighbourhood

Godrej Hill is a residential pocket in Khadakpada, Kalyan West, known for newer high-rise housing, wider roads and greener surroundings than the older town centre. Khadakpada as a whole has grown into one of the preferred residential areas of Kalyan West, with schools, hospitals, supermarkets, cinemas and restaurants within a few minutes.

Connectivity today and tomorrow

Today, residents of Khadakpada reach Kalyan station by road for suburban trains towards Thane and Mumbai, and use the Kalyan–Murbad and Kalyan–Bhiwandi roads for travel by car. For many families on Godrej Hill, the daily trip that matters most is the school run, and the cluster of schools within about a kilometre is a real advantage.

Looking ahead, the approved Metro Line 5A plan includes a station at Khadakpada on the route from Durgadi towards Kalyan, with an interchange planned with Metro Line 12 towards Navi Mumbai. Officials have targeted the extended corridor for around 2030–31, so it is a long-term benefit. Our guide to upcoming infrastructure near Kalyan sets out what is approved and what is still planned.
