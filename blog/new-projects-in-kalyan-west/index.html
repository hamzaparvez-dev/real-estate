Why new projects in Kalyan West are in demand

Kalyan West has become one of the busiest housing markets on the Central Railway line. It offers a major railway junction, an established city with schools and hospitals, and prices that are usually lower than in Thane or Mumbai. Planned transport links such as Metro Line 5 and 5A have added to the interest, and new towers have come up across the city.

That choice is good for buyers, but it also means a lot of advertising. A clear, step-by-step way of comparing projects protects you from paying for promises that are not in writing.

Where new projects are coming up

Buyers searching for new projects in Kalyan West typically look at a few clusters. The central area around Kala Talao and the station offers convenience and established markets. Khadakpada and Godrej Hill are quieter and more residential, with schools and hospitals close by. Other localities such as Gandhare, Barave, Adharwadi and the Birla College road area also feature in searches, each with its own trade-offs of price, distance and road access.

Do not choose a locality from a map alone. Spend time there at the hour you would leave for work and the hour you would come home.

  • Kala Talao and station area: central, close to rail and markets
  • Khadakpada and Godrej Hill: residential, schools and hospitals nearby
  • Other localities: compare price, distance and road access yourself

Step 1: check MahaRERA before anything else

Every residential project above the size thresholds in the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act must be registered with MahaRERA before it is advertised or sold. Search for the project on the MahaRERA portal, note the registration number, and check the promoter's name, the proposed completion date, the approved plans and the quarterly progress updates.

If a project's advertising does not show a registration number, or the number does not match the project name and location on the portal, treat that as a warning sign. Our step-by-step guide on how to check a MahaRERA registration number shows where to click.

  • Registration number shown on the advert and on the portal
  • Promoter name and the project's legal address
  • Proposed completion date and any extensions
  • Approved plans and quarterly progress reports

Step 2: compare carpet area, not super built-up

Since RERA, projects must sell on RERA carpet area: the usable floor area inside your walls. Some advertisements still mention built-up or super built-up areas, which include walls, balconies or a share of common areas and so look bigger. Always ask for the RERA carpet area in writing and use it to compare price per square foot across projects.

For example, Prestige Gold lists its 1 BHK at 404–519 sq ft and its 2 BHK at 577–721 sq ft of RERA carpet area, so you can compare like with like.

Step 3: understand the possession date

Under-construction homes come with a possession date, and it should match the completion date declared on MahaRERA. Ask what happens if possession is delayed, and read the clause in the agreement. Check the quarterly progress updates on MahaRERA, and visit the site more than once to see how work is moving.

Prestige Gold's possession is targeted for June 2027, subject to the MahaRERA timeline. Our comparison of under-construction vs ready-to-move homes explains the trade-offs of waiting.

Step 4: read the payment plan

Most new projects use a construction-linked payment plan, where you pay instalments as the building reaches each stage. Ask for the schedule in writing, check that each instalment is tied to a stage you can see on site, and confirm how GST, stamp duty, registration and other charges are collected.

Be careful with schemes that promise very low payments now and a large lump sum later; make sure you understand who pays the interest and what happens if the project is delayed.

  • Booking amount and when the agreement will be registered
  • Construction stages linked to each instalment
  • GST, stamp duty and registration: when and how much
  • Parking, society and maintenance charges

Step 5: look at the developer and the details

Look at what the developer has completed before, and visit an older building if you can to see how it has aged. Then look at the details of the new project: the specifications, the number of lifts per floor, water storage, power backup, parking and the open space. Ask for a written specification sheet as part of the agreement.

Talk to people who already live nearby about water supply, monsoon drainage and traffic. They will tell you things that a brochure will not.

Red flags to watch for

Most projects are run honestly, but a few warning signs should make you slow down. Be wary of pressure to pay a booking amount the same day, offers that are only valid in cash, and promises about views, amenities or possession dates that the sales team will not put in writing. Be equally careful if the project name, promoter or address on the advert does not match MahaRERA, or if the quarterly progress updates on the portal have stopped.

If something feels rushed, step back and check. A genuine offer will still be there after you have read the documents.

  • Pressure to pay a booking amount the same day
  • Cash-only offers or unclear receipts
  • Promises that are not written into the agreement
  • Advert details that do not match the MahaRERA portal
  • Progress updates on MahaRERA that have stopped

Questions people ask

How do I check if a new project in Kalyan West is RERA registered?
Search for the project on the MahaRERA portal and match the registration number, project name, promoter and address with the advertisement.
What should I compare between new projects?
RERA carpet area, price per sq ft of carpet area, possession date on MahaRERA, payment plan, specifications and the total cost including taxes and charges.
Is GST charged on new flats in Kalyan West?
GST applies to under-construction homes. It does not apply to ready homes that already have an occupancy certificate. Confirm the rate on your cost sheet.
