Metro Line 5 & 5A in Kalyan: Stations, Phases and Timelines

By Prestige Projects · Updated November 26, 2026 · 9 min read

Quick answer

Metro Line 5 links Thane, Bhiwandi and Kalyan. Phase 1, from Balkum in Thane to Dhamankar Naka in Bhiwandi, is about 11.9 km with six stations and is targeted to open around December 2026. Phase 2 continues about 10.48 km to Durgadi in Kalyan. The 5A extension, approved by the Centre in August 2026, runs about 11.83 km from Durgadi to Ulhasnagar with stations including Khadakpada and Kalyan, and is targeted for around 2030–31.

Why Metro Line 5 matters for Kalyan

For decades, getting in and out of Kalyan has meant the suburban railway or the roads towards Thane and Bhiwandi. The local train is fast but crowded, and road journeys at peak hours can be slow. Metro Line 5 is designed to add a third option: an elevated metro running from Thane through Bhiwandi to Kalyan, and with the 5A extension, on to Ulhasnagar.

For people living in Kalyan West, especially around Durgadi, Khadakpada and the station area, the line could eventually offer a direct metro connection to Thane and to other metro lines across the region. This guide sets out what has been announced and when, so you can judge it realistically.

Phase 1: Balkum (Thane) to Dhamankar Naka (Bhiwandi)

The first phase runs from Balkum in Thane to Dhamankar Naka in Bhiwandi. According to an MMRDA press release of 28 May 2026, it is about 11.9 km long with six stations, and its opening was targeted for around December 2026. Opening dates for metro lines often move, so check MMRDA's latest announcements before relying on a date.

This phase does not reach Kalyan itself, but it is the first step of the corridor and will connect Bhiwandi to Thane.

  • Route: Balkum (Thane) to Dhamankar Naka (Bhiwandi)
  • Length: about 11.9 km, six stations
  • Target: around December 2026 (per MMRDA, May 2026)

Phase 2: Dhamankar Naka to Durgadi (Kalyan)

Phase 2 continues the line about 10.48 km from Dhamankar Naka to Durgadi in Kalyan, with an estimated cost of about ₹7,326 crore. Durgadi, near the creek on the western edge of Kalyan, is where Line 5 enters the city.

Firm opening dates for this phase have not been confirmed, so treat it as a medium-term project.

Metro 5A: Durgadi to Ulhasnagar

The 5A extension carries the metro through Kalyan to Ulhasnagar. As reported by the Indian Express on 31 August 2026, the Centre approved the extension on 29 August 2026. It is about 11.83 km long, with an estimated cost of about ₹4,063 crore, and construction is targeted for completion around 2030–31.

The reported stations are listed below. The line is planned to interchange with Metro Line 12 at Kalyan, which would link Kalyan with other parts of the metro network.

  • Durgadi
  • Khadakpada
  • Bhoirwadi
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Path
  • Kalyan (interchange with Metro Line 12)
  • Shanti Nagar
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Chowk
  • Ulhasnagar

Metro Line 12 and the Kalyan interchange

Metro Line 12 is planned to run from Kalyan towards Taloja in Navi Mumbai. The Kalyan interchange between Line 12 and Line 5A is what could turn Kalyan from the end of several lines into a junction of the metro network, in the same way that Kalyan is already a junction of the suburban railway.

For buyers, the practical point is that a metro journey from Kalyan West could, in future, connect towards Thane and Bhiwandi on one side and Navi Mumbai on the other. As with the other lines, check MMRDA updates for the current status and expected dates before you factor it in.

What it could mean for Khadakpada and Godrej Hill

Khadakpada is one of the planned 5A stations. For residents of Khadakpada and Godrej Hill, a metro station within the neighbourhood could, once built, offer an alternative to taking a rickshaw to Kalyan station, and a direct link towards Durgadi and, through Line 5, to Bhiwandi and Thane.

Prestige One, at Godrej Hill, Khadakpada, is in this part of Kalyan West. But the station is part of a project targeted for around 2030–31, so buy a home here because it suits you today, and treat the metro as a possible future benefit. What it could mean for central Kalyan and Kala Talao

The 5A route also includes a Kalyan station with an interchange to Metro Line 12, and stations such as Chhatrapati Shivaji Path and Shanti Nagar. For homes in the central area, such as Prestige Gold near Kala Talao, that could add metro connections to the suburban railway already close by.

As with Khadakpada, these benefits depend on construction timelines. Today, the main advantage of central Kalyan West is the short distance to Kalyan Junction. Our guide to 1 BHK and 2 BHK flats near Kala Talao explains what living there is like now.

The rail network you can use today

Until the metro arrives, the suburban railway remains the backbone of daily travel from Kalyan. Kalyan Junction is served by slow and fast local trains towards Thane and Mumbai, and by trains towards Kasara and Karjat, and many long-distance trains stop there too.

When you look at a home, time the journey to the station at the hour you would actually leave, and check which trains you can realistically catch. A flat that is ten minutes from the station by rickshaw at noon can take much longer at 8.30 in the morning.

  • Slow and fast locals towards Thane and Mumbai
  • Trains towards Kasara and Karjat
  • Many long-distance trains stop at Kalyan Junction

How to think about infrastructure when you buy

Infrastructure projects can change how a city works, but they take time and their timelines often move. A sensible approach is to choose a home that works for your family with today's transport, and to treat a future metro line as a bonus rather than the main reason to buy. Be careful of advertising that describes a project as "next to the metro" when the station is years away.

If you are buying as an investment, remember that construction itself can bring years of road diversions and dust along the route before the benefits arrive.

  • Buy for how you will live and travel today
  • Treat announced lines as future, not current, benefits
  • Check official MMRDA updates, not just adverts
  • Expect disruption along the route during construction

Questions to ask about infrastructure claims

Property advertising often mentions upcoming metro lines, roads and bridges. These can be real and useful, but the details matter. Ask which project is being referred to, whether it has been approved or is only proposed, who is building it, and what the latest official completion date is.

Then ask how far the nearest station or access point will be from the building you are looking at, in walking or driving distance, and check it yourself on a map.

  • Which project is meant, and is it approved or only proposed?
  • Who is building it, and what is the latest official date?
  • How far will the nearest station be from this building?

Where to check the latest updates

For the latest information on Metro Line 5 and 5A, check announcements from MMRDA, the agency building the line, and reports in reliable newspapers. Station names, alignments and dates can change between approval and construction, so look for the most recent official update rather than older news.

When you look at a property, ask the seller where exactly the nearest planned station is, and measure the distance yourself on a map.
