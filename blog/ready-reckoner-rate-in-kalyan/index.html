Ready Reckoner Rate in Kalyan: How to Check the RR Rate

By Prestige Projects · Updated November 26, 2026 · 9 min read

Quick answer

The ready reckoner (RR) rate is the government's minimum value per square metre for property in each zone. For Kalyan, look it up on the Maharashtra IGR website under eASR: choose the year, Thane district, Kalyan taluka and then your village or municipal zone. Stamp duty is charged on the higher of your agreement value and the RR value, so the RR rate matters even when you pay more than it.

What the ready reckoner rate is

The ready reckoner rate, also called the RR rate, circle rate or Annual Statement of Rates (ASR), is a minimum value per square metre that the Maharashtra government sets for land and buildings in each area. It is published by the Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR Maharashtra) and is usually revised at the start of the financial year, though the state can leave rates unchanged.

The rate differs by location and by type of property. Within Kalyan, open land, residential flats, shops and offices each have their own rate, and the rate can differ from one zone or sub-zone to the next.

Why the RR rate matters when you buy in Kalyan

When you register a purchase, stamp duty is charged on the higher of two figures: the value in your agreement and the value worked out from the ready reckoner rate. If you agree a price below the RR value, you still pay duty on the RR value. If your price is above it, as is common for new projects, you pay duty on your price.

The RR rate also matters for tax. When a property is sold for less than its stamp duty value, income tax rules can treat the stamp duty value as the sale price, beyond a small tolerance. Our guide on capital gains tax on a property sale explains the tax side.

  • Stamp duty is charged on the higher of agreement value and RR value
  • A price far below the RR value is a reason to ask questions
  • Income tax rules can use the stamp duty value for buyer and seller

How to check the RR rate for Kalyan on eASR

The official rates are on the IGR Maharashtra website, in the section called eASR or Annual Statement of Rates. The steps below are how the search is usually laid out; the exact menus can change from year to year.

If you are unsure which zone your building falls in, the survey or CTS number on the property's documents, or the sub-registrar's office, can help you pin it down.

  • Open the IGR Maharashtra website and choose eASR (Annual Statement of Rates)
  • Select the financial year
  • Select Thane district and Kalyan taluka
  • Select the village, or the Kalyan-Dombivli Municipal Corporation area and zone
  • Find your sub-zone by survey or CTS number and read the rate for residential flats

Reading the rate: per square metre and built-up area

eASR rates are given per square metre, while flats in Kalyan are usually discussed in square feet. To convert a rate per square metre into a rate per square foot, divide by 10.764. For example, a rate of ₹60,000 per square metre is about ₹5,574 per square foot.

The guidelines published with eASR also explain how to apply the rate to a flat. They usually work on built-up area, and give a factor for converting carpet area into built-up area, often 1.2. They also set adjustments, for example for higher floors in buildings with lifts and for the age of older buildings. Read the current year's guidelines before you rely on a calculation.

A worked example

Here is an illustration with made-up figures; it is not the actual rate for any part of Kalyan. Suppose the RR rate for your sub-zone is ₹60,000 per square metre and your flat has 500 sq ft of RERA carpet area. 500 sq ft is about 46.45 sq m; at a 1.2 factor, the built-up area is about 55.74 sq m; so the RR value is about ₹33.4 Lakh before any floor or age adjustments.

If your agreement value is ₹55 Lakh, stamp duty is charged on ₹55 Lakh, because that is higher. If you were buying an older resale flat for ₹30 Lakh, duty would be charged on the RR value of about ₹33.4 Lakh instead.

  • Carpet area: 500 sq ft ≈ 46.45 sq m
  • Built-up at 1.2: ≈ 55.74 sq m
  • Illustrative RR value at ₹60,000/sq m: ≈ ₹33.4 Lakh
  • Duty base: the higher of this and your agreement value

Common mistakes with the RR rate

The most common mistake is using the wrong rate: a rate for open land instead of residential flats, a rate from last year, or a rate for a neighbouring zone. The second is applying a per square metre rate to square feet without converting, which makes the value look more than ten times too high. The third is forgetting the built-up area factor and the floor or age adjustments in the guidelines.

If your own calculation gives a very different answer from the one on the sub-registrar's valuation, ask how they arrived at theirs before you pay.

  • Using the land rate instead of the residential flat rate
  • Using last year's rate or a neighbouring zone
  • Mixing up square metres and square feet
  • Ignoring the built-up factor and floor or age adjustments

Kala Talao, Khadakpada and other zones

Different parts of Kalyan West fall into different zones, so a flat near Kala Talao and a flat in Khadakpada or Godrej Hill can have different RR rates even if their market prices look similar. Do not use a rate from a listing website or a neighbour's flat; look up the exact zone for your building.

RR rate and stamp duty in Kalyan

Once you know the duty base, apply the stamp duty rate. In Mumbai, the rate for residential property is 6%, or 5% for a woman buying in her sole name, including a 1% metro cess. In other municipal corporation areas such as Kalyan-Dombivli, a local body surcharge can apply as well, and the total is commonly about 7%; confirm the exact rate with the sub-registrar or on the IGR website before you budget. The registration fee is 1% of the value, capped at ₹30,000.

RR rate is not the market price

The ready reckoner rate is a floor for stamp duty, not a guide to what a flat is worth. Market prices in Kalyan West depend on the building, the developer, the floor and view, amenities, age and how close you are to the station or schools. New projects are often priced well above the RR value, while some older resale flats trade close to it.

If a seller offers a flat at a price well below the RR value, find out why. It can point to a problem with the title, the building's condition or the society's dues. Our documents to check before buying property checklist helps.

When rates change

Rates are normally announced for each financial year, starting 1 April. If you are close to registering a purchase around that date, check which year's rate will apply on the day you register, because a change can alter the duty on a resale flat priced near the RR value. For under-construction homes registered at the agreement stage, the agreement value is usually higher than the RR value anyway.

Questions people ask

Where can I check the ready reckoner rate for Kalyan?
On the IGR Maharashtra website under eASR (Annual Statement of Rates). Choose the year, Thane district, Kalyan taluka and then your village or municipal zone.
Is the RR rate given per square foot?
No. eASR rates are per square metre. Divide by 10.764 to get an approximate rate per square foot.
Is stamp duty charged on the RR value or my price?
On whichever is higher: the agreement value or the value worked out from the RR rate.
How often does the ready reckoner rate change?
It is usually announced for each financial year from 1 April, though the state can keep rates unchanged.
Is the RR rate the same as the market price?
No. It is a minimum value for stamp duty. Market prices depend on the building, location, floor, amenities and demand.
