Questions to ask any builder or agent in Kalyan

Whether you are speaking to us or to anyone else, a few direct questions will tell you quickly whether a project and the people selling it are worth your time. Good answers are specific and come in writing; vague answers are a reason to slow down.

Ask first about the registration and the timeline. Every new project in Maharashtra that needs registration should have a MahaRERA number, and the filing shows the promoter, the approved plan, the declared completion date and quarterly progress. Ask what the date in your agreement will be, and compare it with the date on the filing.

Then ask about money. Request a written cost sheet for one specific unit, not a range, and ask which charges are inside the quoted price and which are extra: parking, stamp duty, registration, GST, society formation, maintenance deposit, legal charges and any floor-rise or view premium. Ask how payments are linked to construction stages, and never pay in cash.

Finally, ask to see what you are buying. A sample flat, the floor plan with RERA carpet area, the specification sheet and a walk around the actual site tell you more than any brochure. At Prestige Gold a sample flat is ready to visit; at Prestige One we share layouts privately and walk you through the building.

  • What is the MahaRERA registration number, and who is the promoter on the filing?
  • What possession date will be written in my agreement?
  • Can I have a written cost sheet for this exact unit?
  • Which charges are not included in the quoted price?
  • Can I see the sample flat or a finished floor before I pay a token?
